# Provenance

The corpus creator stated in the source conversation that the corpus was synthetic and generated by them. That is operator-reported provenance, not an independent determination of how the data was generated.

The supplied files are `pages.jsonl`, `revisions.jsonl`, `events.jsonl`, `labels.jsonl` and `manifest.json`. Their expanded bytes match all five supplied SHA-256 checksums. Repository copies use gzip compression; decompression restores those bytes without editing them.

Names, labels, URLs, dates and claims inside the corpus are source content. Their presence does not authenticate the depicted events, identify real actors or establish that a URL was fetched. No generation recipe, execution harness or independent authentication of real events was supplied for this publication.

Analysis code and reports were produced with Codex assistance. Measurements are reproducible under the published parsers. Heuristic classifications remain open to inspection and correction. SHA-256 verifies byte identity, not factual truth.

The original byte-identical-adjacent-page metric was rejected as a test of functional repetition. The current audits group declared operations, retain transport differences and inspect subsequent written states. Neither audit converts a page-save event into successful execution of a link appearing on that page.

This repository publishes the synthetic source corpus, analysis and reproducibility material. It does not publish the private support conversation or unrelated account information discussed during the session.
